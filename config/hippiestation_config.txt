## Discord admin commands through TGS (status, ahelp, namecheck, adminwho, sdql) only run for a Discord account linked
## to a game admin with the rights: Link Discord Account in game, then "verify <ckey>" in Discord. The links are kept in
## the database, so with no database these commands are refused. 0 lets anyone who can post in an admin channel use them.
#SECURE_CHAT_COMMANDS 0

## Tops the crew up to this many with bot crew members, players counted first. Unset or 0 is off.
## Can also be changed mid-round with the "Set Bot Crew Target" admin verb.
## They start at their posts with everyone else when the round starts.
BOT_CREW_TARGET 20

## How many more bots come in on the arrivals shuttle later on, picked at random between these two.
#BOT_CREW_LATE_MIN 5
#BOT_CREW_LATE_MAX 10

## How many of those bots turn out to be traitors, ten minutes or so into the round. Modes only pick antagonists from
## players, so a crew of bots has none without this. Unset or 0 is none.
BOT_CREW_TRAITORS 2

## How much trouble the bot crew gets up to by itself: 0 none, 1 a normal round (greytide, quarrels, the odd riot), 2 mayhem.
## Unset is 1. Headless test runs leave it off unless they ask for it with bot-chaos=<level>.
#BOT_CHAOS 1

## How well the bot crew gets itself out of a fix, uses what a room offers against somebody and reads what's about to hurt it,
## 0-100 on average. Most play like ordinary players at the default; robust bots and powergamers roll higher. Unset is 40.
## Admins can set one bot's, or everyone's, in the round with the "Set Bot Skill" verb.
#BOT_SKILL_MEAN 40

## Out of a hundred vigilante-minded bots (antag hunters, robust, paranoid), how many play at apex (90 and up). Unset is 15.
#BOT_APEX_SHARE 15

## 1: bot antagonists play at apex, every trick set up on purpose. 0: at the skill they rolled. Unset is 1.
#BOT_ANTAGS_APEX 1

## Write the bots' telemetry (data/logs/<round>/bot_telemetry.jsonl: where each bot is, what it holds and does, twice a
## second) in a live round too. Test runs always write it. Also on whenever PLAYER_LOG_CKEY is set.
#BOT_TELEMETRY

## Station looks: how often each one comes up against the others in its slot. 0 means never; unset ones keep these weights.
## Shift time (one every round): day 35, morning 20, night 30, power_saving 15.
## Sky (one every round): sky_quiet 20; sky_ember, sky_glacier, sky_violet, sky_verdant, sky_gold, sky_rose 10 each.
## Extras (up to two): tired_wiring 10, and none_extra 10 for rolling none. History (at most one): none_history 20.
## Admins can force, ban and re-roll them in the round with the Station Looks verb.
#STATION_LOOK_WEIGHT night 30
#STATION_LOOK_WEIGHT none_extra 10

## One player's actions get logged the way the bots' are, in data/logs/<round>/player_telemetry.jsonl: where they are, what they
