#   <sheet>: <lit state> [<lit state> ...] bright[=N]         no off state: the pixels at least N bright (default 200)

#   ... changed                                                also coloured pixels that clearly differ, brighter or not
#   <sheet>: *<suffix> - *<other suffix>                       every state that has an off twin by that pattern ("* - *-off")
